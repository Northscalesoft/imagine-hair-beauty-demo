A Neighborhood Salon

Imagine Hair and Beauty is a family-friendly salon in the Warm Springs plaza in Fremont. Our stylists work with all hair types and are known for careful, unhurried work — especially short cuts and color. The team speaks English, Mandarin, Taiwanese and Cantonese, and kids are always welcome.
